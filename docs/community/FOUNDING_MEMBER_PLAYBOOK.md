# Founding members: an honest start

This is an invitation plan, not evidence of existing activity. Never manufacture
accounts, reviews, testimonials, photos, Helpful votes or participant numbers.
Participation is voluntary; obtain permission before reusing anyone’s content.

## Three invitations

- **Founding Local:** someone living in Korea who wants to share considerate,
  practical knowledge. First mission: a transport Tip or a neighborhood Moment.
- **Founding Traveler:** someone planning or reflecting on a real visit. First
  mission: a first-day Story, a genuine place Review, or an Ask a Local question.
- **Founding K-Culture Fan:** someone connecting cultural interests with real
  experiences. First mission: a K-pop trip Story or respectful visitor Tip.

These are invitation themes, not account roles, public badges or privileges.
Do not promise points, ranking, free events or special moderation treatment.

## First contribution missions

1. Share one photo you own and a short caption; avoid faces and precise private locations.
2. Tell one true experience with a useful takeaway, not an advertorial.
3. Offer one specific practical tip, noting that conditions can change.
4. Ask a question you genuinely need answered, or answer one you know well.
5. Review only a place you actually visited; disclose any relevant connection.

## Twenty starter prompts

1. What surprised you on your first day in Korea?
2. What do you wish you had packed?
3. Which small street detail caught your eye?
4. What helped you navigate your first subway transfer?
5. What did you learn from your first market visit?
6. Where did you find a quiet moment in Seoul?
7. What was a considerate way to ask for help?
8. What café custom was new to you?
9. What did a rainy day teach you about your itinerary?
10. What Korean phrase made a difference?
11. What would you do differently on your second trip?
12. What was your favorite walk and why?
13. What helped you feel welcome?
14. What did you discover beyond a filming location?
15. How did you plan a respectful K-pop-themed day?
16. What food would you recommend trying thoughtfully?
17. What accessibility detail helped you plan?
18. What did you enjoy that did not cost much?
19. What did you learn about shopping etiquette?
20. What would you tell a nervous first-time visitor?

## Moderation routine

Open `/admin/community` and review oldest pending items daily. Verify that the
photo and text are appropriate together. Check permission, personal information,
harassment, promotional spam, unsafe advice and private meeting details. Do not
publish identities from private profiles. Approve or reject; publication is
one-way in this MVP and members cannot edit submitted content. Feature only an
already approved item for usefulness and variety, not status or connections.

Read reports daily. Resolve only after actually reviewing; the button records
review, not removal. Escalate harmful published material to the operator: this
sprint does not introduce deletion/unpublishing or a takedown UI. Establish an
operator support and lawful takedown process before broad public recruitment.
Use existing question/answer/review queues separately. Never change a member’s
role as part of founding-member recruitment.

## Launch week

- Day 1: invite a small opt-in mix from all three groups; explain moderation/privacy.
- Day 2: help members make one real contribution; observe mobile photo selection.
- Day 3: review submissions; choose the first prompt based on genuine questions.
- Day 4: feature a small variety of approved contributions with permission context.
- Day 5: invite members to read and mark genuinely useful posts Helpful.
- Day 6: answer practical questions and review reports; record friction privately.
- Day 7: thank contributors without publishing private names; choose next week’s prompt.

Measure actual approved contributions, review turnaround and unanswered questions.
Use aggregate observations; do not publish member-level activity or fake momentum.
