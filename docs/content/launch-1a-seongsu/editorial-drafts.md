## 4. Why Seongsu Became Seoul's Creative Neighborhood

Role: K-Contents / culture / discovery. Planning slug: seongsu-creative-neighborhood.

The useful question is not whether Seongsu deserves a fashionable nickname. It is how to look at the neighborhood without reducing it to one.

### Begin with work
Korea Tourism Organization's account of Seongsu's handmade-shoe district traces the gathering of shoemakers to the 1970s [C01]. That gives a visitor a concrete historical thread. It does not mean every old-looking building was a shoe factory, or that a particular business has operated continuously since that period.

Look for verified interpretation, not an invented backstory. A brick wall alone cannot tell you who worked behind it. If you want to name a building's original use, research that building separately and ask permission before entering an active workplace.

### Notice the layers, without inventing the cause
Seoul's official tourism description connects Yeonmujang-gil's industrial traces with a present-day mix of cafés, shops and pop-ups [C02]. It supports describing a contrast. It does not, by itself, prove why rents changed, who benefited, or what every resident thinks.

The title's “became” question therefore needs a careful answer: this draft documents a historical trade and a tourism-described retail layer, not a complete causal history. Claims about redevelopment, artists, displacement or the effect of social media remain research questions [C06]. Do not fill those gaps with a neat story.

### Make a culture visit attentive
Spend part of the walk noticing materials, signs and how space is used. Do not turn workers or residents into background characters for a travel video. Ask before interviews and explain where the recording may appear. A refusal is not an obstacle to work around.

### Bring the screen into perspective
If Korean music, drama or design first made you curious about Seoul, use that curiosity as a doorway rather than pretending a street represents all of Korea. The existing K-pop travel article offers that broader connection. No artist visit, filming location or brand relationship is claimed here.
